What you will complete

Six tasks take you from your allotment letter to a confirmed seat.

  1. 1
    CAP details
    Enter your application ID, allotted college, branch, round and status.
  2. 2
    CAP allotment acceptance
    Record that you accepted your seat on the CAP portal.
  3. 3
    Admission form
    Track the college admission form from blank to submitted.
  4. 4
    Document preparation
    Know when your originals and photocopies are ready.
  5. 5
    Physical verification
    Done in person at the college. You only record the outcome here.
  6. 6
    Fee and payment status
    Mark the fee as not paid, partly paid or paid.
